## 2. DOSHISHA Electric Shaved Ice Machine

**Made in Japan** — designed and manufactured domestically, not just a Japanese brand name slapped on an imported shell. This is the machine behind Japan's "fuwafuwa" (fluffy) shaved-ice trend, producing snow-fine ice instead of the hard, crunchy shards a basic ice shaver makes — genuinely closer to what you'd get at a proper kakigori shop than anything else you can plug in at home. Reviews on Amazon.co.jp are consistently positive, and the honest caveats are minor and specific: the motor runs a bit louder than you'd expect, and the included ice cup is on the small side if you're making a crowd's worth in one go.
