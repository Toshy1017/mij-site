**Made in Japan.** Tokoname is one of Japan's Six Ancient Kilns, and its clay has a real functional property that matters here: it's naturally iron-rich, which softens sencha's bitterness and rounds out the brew in a way porcelain simply can't. This is a genuinely trusted category in Japan itself — Tokoname teapots from named individual artisans consistently pull 3 to 5 star ratings on Amazon.co.jp, the kind of spread you'd expect from handmade pieces with real character rather than mass-produced uniformity. If you've only ever brewed sencha in a Western teapot, this is the upgrade that actually changes the cup.
