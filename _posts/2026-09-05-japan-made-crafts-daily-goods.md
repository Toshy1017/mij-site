Worth stating plainly: Amazon.com's own rating sits at a mixed 3.3 out of 5 across 8 ratings, with a notable share of 1-star reviews — small review counts can swing hard for a low-cost item like this, but it's not a universally loved listing.

## 8. Mejiro Co. Kintsugi Repair Kit with Low-Allergenic Japanese Urushi Lacquer

**Made in Japan** — the listing states "AUTHENTIC KINTSUGI REPAIR KIT FROM TOKYO, JAPAN." Currently unavailable on Amazon.com. 4.1 out of 5 across 13 ratings, which is solid, but there's a safety note worth stating plainly rather than burying: this kit uses real urushi lacquer, and at least one verified buyer reported a serious allergic skin reaction requiring medical treatment despite wearing gloves — urushiol is chemically related to poison ivy's active irritant. The "low allergenic" formulation reduces but doesn't eliminate that risk, which matters for a craft kit aimed at general hobbyists, not just specialists who already know to be careful with urushi.
