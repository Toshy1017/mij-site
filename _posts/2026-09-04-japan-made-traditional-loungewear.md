Jinbei, samue, and hanten all started as workwear — for summer heat, for artisans and potters who needed to move freely, for staying warm through a winter without central heating — before becoming some of the most genuinely comfortable loungewear you can buy. These three come from Japan's actual cotton textile regions, not a generic "Japanese-style" import line.

## 1. WATANOSATO Jinbei (Cotton Crepe, Kurume)

**Made in Japan**, explicitly labeled. WATANOSATO is based in Kurume, a Fukuoka region with a genuine, long-standing reputation for high-quality cotton textiles — not a coincidence, but the actual reason a loungewear specialist set up there. This jinbei uses a cotton crepe weave, the traditional lightweight summer construction that jinbei were originally designed around: loose, breathable, built for heat rather than looks. WATANOSATO carries multiple confirmed Made-in-Japan lines on Amazon, which is a meaningfully different signal than a single import listing — it points to an established regional specialist rather than a one-off product.

## 2. WATANOSATO Hiroshima Denim Samue

**Made in Japan**, same maker as above. Samue started as artisan workwear — potters, priests, sculptors, anyone who needed clothing that moved with them rather than against them — and has since become one of Japan's most popular loungewear formats. This version is woven from Hiroshima-region denim fabric, a genuine textile-heritage detail rather than a generic fabric swap, giving it more structure and durability than the typical soft-cotton samue.

## 3. HANTEN Cotton Jacket (Kimono-Style)

**Made in Japan**, explicitly labeled. Hanten is the padded, quilted short kimono-style jacket that generations of Japanese households relied on for warmth before central heating was standard — thick cotton wadding sewn between two layers of fabric, worn over regular clothes rather than as a bathrobe substitute. It's a simpler, less brand-driven pick than the two WATANOSATO pieces above, but a straightforward, honestly-labeled entry point into traditional Japanese loungewear if jinbei and samue don't fit what you're looking for.
